# SOUNDWAVE-RECORD-ANALYSIS

## PROJECT ANALYSIS

SOUNDWAVE RECORD is a music record label looking to use its  data to identify what drives music performance .
This analysis is to analyze soundwave record streaming, audience, marketing &revenue data to identify where the company should invest its marketing budget to maximize  streams, audience engagements & revenue.

## DATA SOURCE

CHATGPT SOUNDWAVE RECORD

## KPIs

Calculated  important KPIs  includes;

1. Total Streams
2. Total_Royalty_Revenue
3. Total_Marketing_Spending
4. Total_Songs_Released
5. Total Artist

## RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Invest more in TikTok and Instagram marketing

TikTok generates the highest number of streams at 74M, followed by Instagram at 58M. They also generate the highest royalty revenue — approximately $340K and $270K respectively.

Recommendation:
Increase investment in TikTok and Instagram campaigns, especially short-form content, artist challenges, music snippets and influencer collaborations.

2. Reduce dependence on the Nigerian market

Nigeria accounts for approximately 129M streams (49.76%) — almost half of SoundWave's total streams.

Recommendation:
Expand marketing campaigns in the UK, Ghana, USA, South Africa, Kenya and Canada to diversify revenue and reduce dependence on one market.

The UK and Ghana are particularly attractive because they already contribute around 28M and 27M streams respectively.

3. Prioritize high-performing artists

Jay Vee leads the artists with about 20M streams, followed by Nova (19M) and Luna Ray (19M).

Recommendation:
Give the top-performing artists more promotional support, including:

Music video promotion
Social media campaigns
Playlist placement
Influencer marketing
Collaborations

However, don't completely ignore lower-performing artists; use targeted campaigns to determine whether they have growth potential.
